Here are some articles and posts I’ve found helpful for me as a graduate computer science student. I mainly use this page as a personal bookmark list, but others may find it useful too.

### Grad school mindset & habits

- [How to avoid procrastination during the research phase of my PhD](https://academia.stackexchange.com/questions/5786/how-to-avoid-procrastination-during-the-research-phase-of-my-phd)  
  Practical strategies for making progress on open-ended research when there are no fixed deadlines.

- [How should I deal with becoming discouraged as a graduate student?](https://academia.stackexchange.com/questions/2219/how-should-i-deal-with-becoming-discouraged-as-a-graduate-student)  
  A good collection of perspectives on handling setbacks, imposter syndrome, and slow progress.

- [Strategies to strengthen self-discipline as a PhD student](https://academia.stackexchange.com/questions/16346/strategies-to-strengthen-self-discipline-as-a-phd-student)  
  Concrete ideas for building consistent work habits and avoiding burnout.

### Learning Resources
- [Causal AI - Book](https://causalai-book.net/)
- [Difference between Autoencoder (AE) and Variational Autoencoder (VAE)](https://towardsdatascience.com/difference-between-autoencoder-ae-and-variational-autoencoder-vae-ed7be1c038f2/)  
  A clear introductory explanation of how VAEs extend standard autoencoders and why that matters.
